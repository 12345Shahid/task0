Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between Staking APY and APR? ▾
Annual Percentage Rate (APR) represents simple interest earned on your principal over one year without compounding. Annual Percentage Yield (APY) accounts for frequency of compounding (daily, weekly, or monthly). By re-staking earned rewards regularly, your compounding APY significantly outperforms the flat APR.
What is Liquid Staking and how does it affect returns? ▾
Liquid staking tokens (such as stETH, JitoSOL, and sAVAX) wrap your deposited principal and accrue staking rewards automatically. They allow you to retain 100% liquidity, trade on secondary markets, or deploy tokens into DeFi lending protocols to earn layered yield.
